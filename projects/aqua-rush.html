Aqua Rush

Mobile infinite runner where a fish dodges sharks and sea rubbish while collecting coins.

Gameplay Programmer

Unity 6, C# / Team of 2 / 2026

Overview

Aqua Rush is an infinite runner for Android phones. You're a fish swimming through the sea, collecting coins while you dodge sharks and the rubbish thrown into the water.

We made it as a team of two for one of our ESAT course projects, using Unity 6 and C#. I programmed the player's movement and the scoreboard, and I took care of most of the game's look: the post-processing, lighting and fog that make it feel underwater, and the light patterns on the seabed.

What I did

Grid movement and input buffer

The fish moves on a 3x3 grid: a swipe in any of the four directions changes its lane or its height. Swipes are buffered, so quick swipes in a row are remembered and played in order instead of being lost while the fish is still moving.

Underwater look

A full post-processing setup, plus lighting and fog tuned so the scene feels deep under the sea. The light patterns on the seabed are a decal projector with a Shader Graph material based on Voronoi noise, imitating the way sunlight ripples through water.

Hit feedback

Hitting an obstacle triggers a second post-processing effect that turns the edges of the screen red, together with a short camera shake, so every hit is felt.

Scoreboard and saving

A simple but effective save system: a few plain data classes and a static class that holds the game data. It loads when the game starts and saves to a JSON file after every run, and the game over screen reads the best scores from it.
